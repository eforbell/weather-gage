# Furutaka provenance

## References used
- U.S. Navy Office of Naval Intelligence recognition material for Japanese heavy cruisers, especially Aoba/Furutaka-family silhouettes available through public-domain U.S. Navy/ONI reproductions on Wikimedia Commons. Used only as high-level visual reference for the raised forecastle, tall Japanese bridge, twin funnels, mast rhythm and aft aircraft-handling area; no raster or drawing is shipped or traced.
- Combined Fleet / Nihon Kaigun Furutaka-class cruiser summaries and tabulated particulars. Used as a public secondary cross-check for the late-1930s modernization context and the approximate principal dimensions used here.
- Scenario source: `src/sim/scenarios.js`, `esperance`, lists Furutaka with `guns: 6` for the October 1942 Cape Esperance battle.

## 1942 facts represented
- Original generated geometry for Furutaka as a fun, readable October 1942 hero ship.
- Modernized Furutaka-class heavy-cruiser proportions: long narrow hull, 181.8 m length, 16.93 m beam and 5.6 m draft in the spec.
- Main battery is scenario-matched: three twin 20.3 cm / 8-inch turrets, two forward with the second superfiring and one aft, for six visible main barrels.
- The silhouette includes IJN-style grey paint, a compact tiered forward bridge, two funnels on uptake casings, fore tripod and aft pole mast cues, boats and a simplified aft aircraft/catapult area.

## Deliberate approximations / departures
- This is an original gameplay hero model, not a measured reconstruction or museum-archive claim. Hull lines, exact bridge-platform shapes, torpedo doors, directors, davits, rails, AA tubs, floatplane details and camouflage are simplified.
- Secondary and small-calibre fittings are decorative only and do not change the scenario main-gun contract.
- The model prioritizes clear 1942 recognition at game distance over exact per-frame Furutaka damage state or every late-refit fitting.
