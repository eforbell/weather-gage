# British 74 (HMS Bellerophon, Marlborough, Russell): provenance and modelling decisions

Original geometry generated from `spec.json` by the headless Blender kit; no
reference image, texture or third-party mesh is shipped.

**Fidelity, not a reconstruction.** One spec stands for the three British
seventy-fours in The Line of Battle. It is derived from [L'Insurgente](../linsurgente/PROVENANCE.md)'s
spec: stretched to 53 m and 14.6 m beam, raised by a second covered gun deck,
with a rig about 8% taller.

## Facts used

- About 53 m long, 14.6 m beam, about 6.6 m draught (rounded).
- 74 guns, matching the scenario: 14 lower-gundeck and 14 upper-gundeck ports a side, plus 9 on the quarterdeck and forecastle.

## Deliberate choices (not evidence)

- **Paint:** black hull with yellow-ochre bands at both gun tiers and black port lids. This is the chequered look the Navy standardised a decade later; in 1794 it was common but not universal.
- **Flags:** the pre-1801 red ensign, defined as a colour pattern in the spec (the kit now accepts a `pattern` for any flag).
- Rig pose, rigging thickness and unmodelled detail as for the Nevis frigates.
