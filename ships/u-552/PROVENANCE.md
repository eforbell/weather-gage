# U-552 — game model provenance

Original generated1942 Type VII-style U-boat for the Wolf Pack scenario. Name, class role and guns0 are from `src/sim/scenarios.js`. Approximate67m×6.2m class proportions are game art, not measured individual-boat data.

The hull, rounded tower, two periscopes, supported rail and stern planes share the U-96 class kit; small tower/scope/paint differences keep the named boats visually coherent. Deck cannon and fine hydroplane/propeller details are deliberately omitted or simplified; no funnel or fictional funnel smoke. No third-party mesh, traced drawing, texture or reference raster is shipped. Not a museum reconstruction.
