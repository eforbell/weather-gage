# USS Baltimore: provenance and modelling decisions

Original geometry generated from `spec.json`; nothing external is shipped.

Baltimore is an **American-style 32-gun frigate derived from
[USS Constellation](../uss-constellation/PROVENANCE.md)**: the same spec scaled
to 42 m (×0.84) with 12 gundeck ports a side plus 4 on the spar deck, matching
`guns: 32` in the Nevis scenario. The historical Baltimore (1798) was a
converted merchantman of about 20 guns; the scenario's 32-gun "Frigate" is a
game abstraction, so the model follows the scenario, not the ship. Paint, flags
and rig pose are Constellation's.
