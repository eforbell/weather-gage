# HMS Orion provenance

## References used
- Scientific American, "The New British Dreadnoughts" (1911): contemporary public description of Orion's 10 × 13.5-inch centreline main battery and principal dimensions. URL: https://www.scientificamerican.com/article/the-new-british-dreadnoughts/
- Scenario source: `src/sim/scenarios.js`, Dogger Bank fictional January 1915 setup, where HMS Orion carries `guns: 10`.

## Modelling decisions
- Original generated geometry; no protected drawings were traced and no local reference image is shipped.
- Fleet-fidelity model, not flagship-level reconstruction: length/beam, raised forecastle, two funnels, tripod-like foremast, and five centreline twin turrets are kept for recognisable Orion-class identity.
- Smaller fittings, boats, secondary battery and exact 1915 alterations are simplified for game-distance readability. The midships Q turret is posed forward on a raised barbette above the simplified funnel casing, and the main mast is tucked ahead of that barrel path, so the game-distance model has exposed barrels rather than a historically measured arc.
