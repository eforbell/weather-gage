# USS Cumberland: provenance and modelling decisions

Original geometry generated from `spec.json` by the headless Blender kit; no
reference image, texture or third-party mesh is shipped.

**Side-faithful, not ship-faithful.** She is derived from [USS Congress](../uss-congress/PROVENANCE.md),
scaled to Cumberland's dimensions, with the spar deck taken off: the old
gundeck is the weather deck, which is what her 1855 razee did.

## Facts used

- About 53 m (175 ft) long, 13.4 m (45 ft) beam, about 6.4 m draught (rounded).
- Razeed in 1855 to a single-deck sloop of war; 24 guns in 1862, modelled as 12 broadside ports a side. The two pivot guns are counted among them, not modelled separately. The scenario rates her `battery: 12`, so the spec states `guns: 24`.
- At anchor off Newport News on 8 March 1862, where Virginia rammed and sank her.

## Deliberate choices (not evidence)

- Pose, paint and flags as for Congress. Ports have no lids.
- The rig keeps her frigate masts at their original heights above the waterline.
