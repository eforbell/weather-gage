# Volontaire: provenance and modelling decisions

Original geometry generated from `spec.json`; nothing external is shipped.

Volontaire is a **French-style 32-gun frigate derived from
[L'Insurgente](../linsurgente/PROVENANCE.md)**: the same spec scaled to 45 m
(×0.947) with 13 gundeck ports a side plus 3 on the quarterdeck and forecastle,
matching `guns: 32` in the Nevis scenario. Paint, flags, open waist and rig pose
are L'Insurgente's. The scenario's ship is a game abstraction; no specific
historical Volontaire was modelled.
