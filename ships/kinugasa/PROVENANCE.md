# Kinugasa provenance

## References used
- U.S. Navy Office of Naval Intelligence recognition drawings for Aoba-class heavy cruisers, including public-domain reproductions on Wikimedia Commons. Used as high-level visual reference for the two-forward/one-aft turret layout, tall tiered bridge, twin funnels, masts and aft aircraft-handling silhouette; no raster or drawing is shipped or traced.
- Combined Fleet / Nihon Kaigun and Japanese-warship public reference summaries for Aoba-class particulars and late-1930s modernization context. Used as secondary cross-checks for dimensions and class arrangement.
- Scenario source: `src/sim/scenarios.js`, `esperance`, lists Kinugasa with `guns: 6` for the October 1942 Cape Esperance battle.

## 1942 facts represented
- Original generated geometry for Kinugasa as a fun, readable October 1942 hero ship.
- Aoba-class heavy-cruiser proportions: 185.17 m length, 17.56 m beam and 5.66 m draft in the spec.
- Main battery is scenario-matched: three twin 20.3 cm / 8-inch turrets, two forward with the second superfiring and one aft, for six visible main barrels.
- The silhouette includes IJN-style grey paint, a prominent tiered forward bridge, two funnels on uptake casings, fore tripod and aft pole mast cues, boats and a simplified aft aircraft/catapult area.

## Deliberate approximations / departures
- This is an original gameplay hero model, not a measured reconstruction or strict shipyard-plan reproduction. Hull offsets, deckhouse footprints, mast platform geometry, torpedo fittings, AA tubs, rails, davits, floatplane detail and paint finish are simplified.
- Secondary and small-calibre fittings are visual detail only and do not alter the scenario main-gun count.
- The model favors an immediately recognizable Kinugasa/Aoba-family 1942 silhouette at battle-camera distance over exhaustive archival fidelity.
