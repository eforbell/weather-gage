# USS Congress: provenance and modelling decisions

Original geometry generated from `spec.json` by the headless Blender kit; no
reference image, texture or third-party mesh is shipped.

**Side-faithful, not ship-faithful**, like the [Nevis frigates](../uss-constellation/PROVENANCE.md).
She is derived from USS Constellation's spec, scaled to Congress's length and
widened to her beam, then posed at anchor.

## Facts used

- About 54 m (179 ft) long, 14.9 m (47 ft 10 in) beam, about 6.6 m draught (rounded).
- 50 guns in 1862 (ten 8-inch shell guns and forty 32-pounders), modelled as 13 gundeck and 12 spar-deck ports a side. The scenario rates her `battery: 13` (an abstract firepower), so the spec states `guns: 50` for the gun-count test.
- At anchor off Newport News on 8 March 1862.

## Deliberate choices (not evidence)

- **Pose:** at anchor, yards square, every sail furled, no headsails.
- **Paint:** black hull with a white gunport band, a common US Navy scheme of the period, natural masts, black yards, coppered bottom.
- **Flags:** 13-stripe ensign at the stern staff and the main truck (`us-1861`; the 34-star canton reads as plain blue at game distance).
- Hull lines, head, stern and rig follow Constellation's 1799 frigate, not Congress's 1840s lines.
